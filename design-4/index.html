The Method

Four series. One practice.

Primal Moves™ trains stability, strength and mobility through quadrupedal movement — crawling, walking, running, jumping and climbing, practiced in combination.

Series 1

Primal

Functional bodyweight strength, balancing the anterior and posterior chains through push-pull work. Where everyone starts.

Series 2

Moves

Progressive strength & mobility — real capacity in the spine, shoulders, hips and hands.

Series 3

Progressions

Dynamic-to-static skill work and hand-supported transitions. For movers ready to go further.

Series 4

Handstand

Complex sequences and dynamic inversions. Requires a solid foundation in the first three series.
