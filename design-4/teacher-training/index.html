Teacher Training

Become an accredited Primal Method coach.

Primal Method Teacher Training

Learn to teach the method.

The Primal Method Teacher Training is delivered in two modules. Module 1 gives you the foundation and fundamentals of the method; Module 2 turns that understanding into the craft of coaching. Complete both and you're an accredited Primal coach.

Apply to become a coach ↗ Full details on primalmoves.com ↗
The Two Modules

Foundation, then delivery.

Module 1
100 hours · €2,750

Foundation & Fundamentals

80 contact hours plus 20 online. Methodology, functional anatomy, movement patterning, the quadruped and sequencing, community building, adjustment techniques, nervous system regulation and dynamic inversions.

Required before Module 2 — you can't coach the method until you've understood it from the inside.

Module 2
50 hours · €1,250

The Coaches Teacher Training

40 contact hours plus 10 online. Teaching delivery; pace, flow, intensity and sequencing; holding space for a room; and the practical side of building or running a studio.
