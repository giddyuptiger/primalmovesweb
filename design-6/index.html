What is Primal?

Not a gym.
A practice.

Primal is a movement practice built on how the body actually works — crawling, hanging, pushing, pulling, balancing, getting upside down. It trains strength and mobility together, in patterns you'd recognise from being a kid.

But the practice is only half of it. What people stay for is the community — the accountability of a class that expects you, the people you end up eating with afterwards, the events that fill the floor on weekends. Families train here together, and the kids have their own classes on the same floor.

When was the last time you went to a gym where nobody was wearing headphones?
