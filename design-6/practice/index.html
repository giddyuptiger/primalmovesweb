The method

Our Method

What Primal Moves trains, and the four series it is built from.

Main objectives

Stability, strength,
mobility, tone.

The main focus of PRIMAL MOVES™ movement training practice is to develop stability, strength, mobility, and excellent muscle tone through quadrupedal movements and dynamic physical maneuvers that replicate the locomotive characteristics of animals.

Enhancing functional movement
Improving fundamental human movements including crawling, walking, running, jumping, and climbing. Through varied combinations and intensities, participants develop better coordination, balance, and functional capacity.
Core strength & stability
Exercises target the abdomen, back, hips, and pelvis — improving posture, preventing injury, and enhancing athletic performance.
Body awareness
By focusing on movement patterns, sensations, and alignment, practitioners enhance proprioception and movement control.
Natural movement patterns
Crawling, rolling, squatting and other primal movements reconnect us with innate abilities and physical fitness.
The four series

Four series. One practice.

01

Primal
