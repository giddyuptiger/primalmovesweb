What is Primal?

not a gym.
a practice.

Primal is a movement practice built on how the body actually works — crawling, hanging, pushing, pulling, balancing, getting upside down. It trains strength and mobility together, in patterns you'd recognise from being a kid.

But the practice is only half of it. What people stay for is the room: the accountability of a class that expects you, the people you end up eating with afterwards, the events that fill the floor on weekends. Body, mind, community, participation.

Ask ten members what Primal is and you'll get ten answers. That's rather the point.

Meet Cherish

slow down
