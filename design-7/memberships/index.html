Billing, cancellation and the rest of the fine print
Billing
Monthly autopay on the 15th, plus tax. Three-month tiers auto-renew every three months until you cancel; The Nomad is a single month.
Cancelling
Five-day cancellation right under California law. After that, no refunds or prorations. Cancelling a three-month tier early incurs a $100 fee, unless a statutory exception applies.
Missing a class
On unlimited tiers, a no-show or a cancellation inside two hours is a $15 fee. On pass-based tiers, the class is forfeited.
Also
Memberships are non-transferable and subject to our waiver, House Rules and cancellation policy, all referenced at checkout.

Questions

Before you commit.

Can I try before joining?
Yes — that's what the two-week trial is for. Unlimited classes, sauna and cold plunge included. Most people join after it, but you're under no obligation.
Do I need experience?
No. Classes marked “Start here” assume none at all, and every class has a scaled version of whatever's being taught.
Can I freeze or cancel?
Needed Freeze and cancellation terms to be confirmed with the studio.
Is the sauna and cold plunge included?
Yes — with membership and with the two-week trial. Recovery is treated as part of the practice, not an upsell.
What if I travel a lot?
The Digital Studio covers you when you're away — live classes and the recorded library, from anywhere.
