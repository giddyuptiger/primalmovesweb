and also

you don't have to
do all of it

Plenty of people buy a day pass, sit in the sauna, drink a coffee, and go home. That is a completely respectable use of forty dollars.

No one here is keeping score. There's no induction, no fitness test, no one hovering to correct your form unless you'd like them to. Come and do as much or as little as you feel like doing.

what is Primal, then?

not a gym.
a practice.

Movement built on how the body actually works — crawling, hanging, pushing, pulling, balancing, getting upside down. Patterns you'd recognise from being a kid, taught so that a complete beginner and a professional acrobat can stand in the same room and both get something out of it.

But the practice is only half of it. What people stay for is the room.

Ask ten members what Primal is and you'll get ten answers. That's rather the point.
