what is Primal, then?

not a gym.
a practice.

Movement built on how the body actually works — crawling, hanging, pushing, pulling, balancing, getting upside down. Patterns you'd recognise from being a kid, taught so that a complete beginner and a professional acrobat can stand in the same room and both get something out of it.

But the practice is only half of it. What people stay for is the community — and families train here together, with the kids on their own classes on the same floor.

When was the last time you went to a gym where nobody was wearing headphones?

Ask ten members what Primal is and you'll get ten answers. That's rather the point.
