The Practice

What Primal actually is, and the method underneath it.

Why Primal exists

A practice, and
the room around it.

Primal is hard to define because it means something different to everyone who does it. We're not going to force it into a fitness category. What we can tell you is the shared foundation — and you'll recognise your own version soon enough.

Primal Moves™ trains stability, strength and mobility through quadrupedal movement — crawling, walking, running, jumping and climbing practiced in combination. It builds coordination, balance and genuine functional capacity. Nick Brewer founded the method in Ibiza in 2016: “Think less, move more, breathe.”

Venice is where it became a club rather than a studio. Eleven thousand square feet, a sauna, a cold plunge, a cafe, and a team of acrobats, stuntwomen and somatic practitioners — plus the events, dinners and gatherings that fill the floor when the classes end.

The method

Four series. One practice.

The Primal method is divided into four series. Compound and Restore run alongside them as strength and recovery work.
