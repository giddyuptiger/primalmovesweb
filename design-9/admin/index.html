Primal Moves Venice

Design studio

Try colours and photographs on the live site, see them in place immediately, then copy the result out for whoever pushes the code.

  1. We’re in design mode, so there’s no passphrase — the EDIT tab shows on every page for everyone. Click it to open the panel.
  2. Browse the site normally — the panel follows you from page to page.
  3. Colour assigns any brand colour to any role, and warns you when a choice becomes hard to read.
  4. Photos puts Swap and Focus on every image. Swap picks a new one; Focus sets which part of a picture stays in frame when it’s cropped.
  5. Copy lets you edit any heading or paragraph straight on the page.
  6. Presets saves a whole look under a name, so we can compare directions.
  7. Hit Copy config and paste it to Jeremy, or into the repo yourself.

Nothing here saves to the website. Everything you change lives in your own browser until the copied config is pasted into the repo and pushed — so experiment as freely as you like. Nobody can break the live site from in here.

The EDIT tab is visible to everyone while we're designing. It gets switched off before launch — one line in config.js.
