Primal Moves Venice

Design studio

Try colours and photographs on the live site, see them in place immediately, then copy the result out for whoever pushes the code.

  1. Enter the passphrase and the studio panel opens.
  2. Browse the site normally — the panel follows you from page to page.
  3. Colour assigns any brand colour to any role, and warns you when a choice becomes hard to read.
  4. Photos puts a Swap button on every image. Pick from the library or upload one to preview.
  5. Hit Copy config and paste it to Jeremy, or into the repo yourself.

Nothing here saves to the website. Everything you change lives in your own browser until the copied config is pasted into the repo and pushed — so you can experiment as freely as you like without anyone else seeing it. This passphrase keeps the panel out of a visitor's way; it isn't a security measure, and it doesn't need to be, because the studio cannot change what anybody else sees.
