## 6. Dashboard (parked until the site ships)

### The counting problem

- MindBody statuses: active, new, reactivated, expired, terminated, suspended. **MindBody does not count paused members. Primal does.**
- True top of funnel = active + paused + staff/investors. MindBody includes staff/investors but drops paused, which understates the number.
- The 134 versus 142 gap is likely **failed auto-pay charges** (roughly 4 declined cards), which sit in an ambiguous state.
- **Policy to define:** a failed payment keeps someone active for 30 days, then flips to expired. Right now nothing defines this and MindBody doesn't move them to pause.
- Lapses between 90-day contracts (someone ends Monday, rejoins Friday) currently read as reactivation. Needs a rule.

### What Gus wants that doesn't exist anywhere

1. A **member roster view**: name, first visit date, membership start, pause history, current status. Clickable, filterable, exportable.
2. Every dashboard metric **clickable through to the underlying list** (expired members, terminated members, at-risk members), with export.
3. A **notes / action plan / resolution field** per member.
4. A **reason-for-leaving code**: moved, injured, construction, price, lost interest. The point is to separate unwinnable churn from winnable churn and spend effort accordingly.
5. **Events revenue** that isn't in Luma (wires, direct bookings). Chanel is working out the process.
6. **Expenses**, ideally a QuickBooks integration, at minimum a weekly or monthly report upload.
7. Texting and outreach **logged** against the member record.

### Guiding principle agreed

Before building further: **list the things the dashboard would do that no existing tool does.** Klaviyo stays the system of record for lifecycle email and SMS. The dashboard covers what Klaviyo can't: MindBody + Toast + Luma in one place, custom status-based triggers, and the operational views above. The ideal end state is that nobody exports a CSV, because the action is already in the tool.

Also noted: 7shifts now separates cafe shifts from event shifts. The events team schedules people into the events section, and time punches follow from there.
